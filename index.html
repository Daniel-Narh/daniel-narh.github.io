Research

Hybrid organic-inorganic halide crystals

I grow crystals by slow solvent evaporation and follow their structure with ATR-FTIR, X-ray diffraction and Raman spectroscopy. Temperature-dependent Raman shows where each crystal changes phase. Work at INTiBS PAN with Prof. Maciej Ptak.

Nitride thin films at the synchrotron

Using reciprocal space maps from ESRF beamline BM02, I analyse epitaxial ScAl(Ga)N films in Python to measure mosaicity and epitaxial quality. Work at IM2NP (CNRS) with Prof. Jacopo Remondina and Dr Thomas Cornelius, and co-author on a manuscript in preparation.

Earlier work

At the University of Ghana I worked on nanomaterials for environmental and biomedical uses: superhydrophobic glass coatings, chitosan/zeolite drug carriers, green-synthesised Ag-TiO2 photocatalysts and natural fibres for oil spill clean-up.
